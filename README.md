# Ironvow

A fantasy RPG where your real daily habits are the quests. Walk, train, meditate, drink water and skip junk food to level up your hero, earn gold and gear, and venture into dangerous lands.

**Play:** https://hariadam.github.io/ironvow/

On iPhone: open the link in Safari → Share → **Add to Home Screen**.

Your hero is saved on your device. Use **Hero → Backup → Copy save code** now and then, and keep the code somewhere safe.

Built entirely with Claude. See [CLAUDE.md](CLAUDE.md) for how the game works.
